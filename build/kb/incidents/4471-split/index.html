The problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Most of our tenants write the day first. The parser reads month first, so for them the day and the month trade places: 7 April goes out as 4 July (R-41200). This is the problem #4471 names, and the fix queued for it.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type month first

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People do what it says, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The symptom is the same flip, but it only happens through the form on booking-v3 tenants (Alder Physio, Copper Studio, Pale Harbour), and the parser fix does not touch it. Intake tags these by the reporter’s browser, so the “tagged as” column disagrees with the cause in both directions.

one-day-move: dates land a day early

These reports are not a flip. The date moves by one day: Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May (R-41202), a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and one in Toronto gets Monday (R-41344), an invite arrives before the booking was made (R-41236), and only June bookings are off by a day (R-41209). That reads as a time zone or daylight saving bug. The desk already listed these as a one-day move; the incident page still counts them under parser by tenant.

How to tell them apart

  1. Does the date move by a day, not swap day and month? one-day-move.
  2. Did it come in through the form of a booking-v3 tenant? hint.
  3. Otherwise, from a tenant that writes the day first: parser.

The subject line tells you none of this. Read the body and look up the tenant.
